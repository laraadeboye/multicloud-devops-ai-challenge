CloudMart is an online Ecommerce company facing issues with IT inefficiency and rising customer support costs. Additionally, it is loosing competition to another e-commerce store that is offering the same products at lower prices.

### CloudMart Decisions- Phase 1
- Redesign and migrate the application to rub in a modern cloud environment with DevOps integrated with AI assistants to reduce customer support costs

### Problems:
- The IT team consists of 30 people including DEvs, Infrastructure Professionals, Database, Security and Managers who are not proficient in multicloud, Devops and AI. A good part of the team, is complacent

- The technology division does not have the budget available to invest in the project.

### Outcome:
- The CTO decides to fire 80% of the team (24 people) to free up budget and hire professionals who are proficient in multicloud, Devops and AI
